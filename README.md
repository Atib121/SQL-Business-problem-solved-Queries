4. E-Commerce Retail (Global)

Project: Real-Time Flash Sale Revenue Leakage & Cart Lifetime Expiration

Summary: Designed a real-time revenue loss auditor to compute Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) leaks and stock-lock timelines by calculating checkout statuses alongside time-difference calculations.

Finding: Uncovered that 61% of total cart value (€13,597) was completely frozen or lost during the flash sale launch window, heavily anchored by Electronics where a hardcoded 15-minute cart expiry rule locked up €9,347 in high-demand items from active buyers.

Recommendation: Transition from static cart holds to dynamic time-throttling (reducing high-demand holds to 5–7 minutes) and trigger urgency alerts at minute 12 to recapture high-value pending checkouts.
